# RootCause: 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script
**AWS First Commit 2026 Presentation Guide**

---

## 0:00 - 0:30: The Problem Statement (Hook)
> *"During crises like hurricanes, grid collapses, or mass-casualty events, resource distribution is chaotic. Responders guess who has supplies, while critical hospitals run dry. Black-box AI algorithms cannot be trusted when lives are on the line, because incident commanders need to know *why* an ambulance or oxygen tanker was dispatched to Hospital A rather than Hospital B.*
>
> *Introducing **RootCause**: a deterministic, explainable, real-time resource allocation platform powered by mathematical precision, strict reserve protection, and Amazon Bedrock explainability."*

---

## 0:30 - 1:15: The Deterministic Engine in Action
1. **Show the Dashboard**:
   - Point to the live KPIs: Active Demands, Available Resources, Confirmed Allocations.
   - Click **"Seed Emergency Scenario"** to load realistic disaster triage supplies across New York hospitals.
2. **Submit an Urgent Demand**:
   - Select "Riverside Clinic" requiring **20 Oxygen Cylinders** under **CRITICAL** urgency.
3. **Execute Matching**:
   - Click **"Run Allocation Engine"**.
   - Show the sub-second result:
     - **Score**: 98.2 / 100
     - **Selected Provider**: Metro General Hospital.
     - **Distance**: 1.2 km (calculated via Haversine great-circle formula).

---

## 1:15 - 2:00: Explainability & Safety Reserve Guarantee
1. **The Transparent Score Breakdown**:
   - Highlight the 5 distinct mathematical scoring components:
     - Resource Compatibility: 30 / 30
     - Urgency Weight: 25 / 25
     - Quantity Availability: 20 / 20
     - Distance Proximity: 14.8 / 15
     - Time Buffer: 8.4 / 10
2. **Safety Reserve**:
   - Show that Metro General Hospital has 10 units locked in reserve that the algorithm *refused* to touch, preventing secondary failure.
3. **Natural Language Narrative**:
   - Show the human-readable summary generated by Amazon Bedrock (or the offline deterministic fallback):
     *"Metro General Hospital was selected due to immediate proximity (1.2 km), full 20-unit fulfillment capability, and preservation of its mandatory 10-unit emergency reserve."*

---

## 2:00 - 2:30: Lifecycle State Machine & Transfer
1. Click **"Confirm Allocation"**:
   - Show that Metro General's inventory immediately decrements from 50 to 30.
   - Show the Demand status advances from `pending` to `matched`.
2. Click **"Complete Transfer"**:
   - Status advances to `fulfilled`.
   - The Activity log updates in real-time.

---

## 2:30 - 3:00: Architecture & AWS Integration (Wrap-up)
> *"RootCause is architected serverless on AWS: AWS Lambda, Amazon API Gateway, Amazon DynamoDB for ultra-low latency, Amazon Bedrock for explainability, and S3 for frontend hosting — fully deployable with one SAM command.*
> *RootCause brings transparency, accountability, and life-saving speed to emergency logistics."*
